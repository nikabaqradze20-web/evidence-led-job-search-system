# Skill: draft cover letter

## Purpose

Draft a vacancy-specific letter only after approval, fit evaluation, evidence selection, and CV preparation exist on disk.

## Nine-slot structure

1. Role and current position.
2. Why this employer.
3. Current work at method level.
4. Prior full-cycle delivery.
5. Tools and one honest gap.
6. Career direction and why the move is deliberate.
7. Sector gap and substantive bridge.
8. Language and availability.
9. Specific close.

Every slot needs a source. If a distinctive employer reason is not available, report that gap instead of inventing praise. The letter is a draft; sending remains outside the workflow.
