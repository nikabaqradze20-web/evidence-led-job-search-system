# Skill: tailor CV

## Purpose

Create a truthful, vacancy-specific one-page CV from approved evidence.

## Editorial rules

- Keep verified names, titles, dates, degrees, grades, and language levels unchanged.
- Put the strongest essential-requirement evidence near the top.
- Use direct evidence first and label transferable bridges honestly.
- Preserve ongoing work as ongoing.
- Keep all completed degrees and meaningful current-role coverage.
- Mirror verified terminology where useful, but never rename the subject matter of prior work.

## Audit

Check source traceability, claim boundaries, bullet allocation, current-role coverage, concrete facts, relevance, banned phrases, and one-page readability before saving.
